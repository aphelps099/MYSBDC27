[Draft copy — replace with the approved SPF posting.] As a Business Advisor, you’ll carry a portfolio of clients across the north coast — bakeries, contractors, retailers, makers — and meet them where they are. Some need a plan. Some need a lender. Most need someone who has done this before to tell them the truth.

What you’ll do

Advise 40–60 clients a year, one-on-one and confidentially, from first conversation through the decision.

Build financial projections, business plans and loan-readiness packages alongside owners.

Bring in network specialists — capital, food and hospitality, government contracting, tech — when the work changes.

Lead the occasional workshop, and document outcomes in MySBDC so the impact is on the record.

Show up in your community — chambers, lenders, economic development partners.

What you bring

Five or more years running, financing or advising small businesses. Owning one counts double.

Comfort with financial statements — you can read a P&L and explain it to someone who can’t.

Plain-spoken, warm and confidential. This is a trust job before it’s a technical one.

Bilingual (Spanish, Hmong, Vietnamese or another regional language) is a real plus, not a checkbox.

“I spent twenty years running restaurants. Now I help other people not make my mistakes.”

A NorCal SBDC advisor, north coast

Benefits

Employed through the Cal Poly Humboldt Sponsored Programs Foundation: health, dental and vision, retirement contributions, paid holidays and leave, professional development and SBDC certification support.
