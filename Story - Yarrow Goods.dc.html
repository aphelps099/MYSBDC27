$138K raised. Sales up 20%.

How the founders of Yarrow Goods turned a values-driven storefront into a funded, growing business — with an advisor at their side.

5 min read
The business Sustainable goods, online and in-store
Worked with A capital advisor + an ecommerce specialist
The outcome $138K raised · Sales up 20% · One new hire
Cost to the business $0 — as always

The story

[Draft copy — replace with the approved story.] When the founders of Yarrow Goods opened their doors, the products moved faster than the paperwork. Growth was real, but so was the question every owner eventually asks: how do we fund the next step without losing the thread?

They signed up for advising. Their advisor started with the business, not a template — inventory, margins, the seasonality of a values-driven retail brand. Together they built the projections and the loan package before approaching a lender.

“Our advisor had actually done this before. Every meeting ended with something we could do that week.”

The result: $138K in financing, a 20% lift in sales the following year, and a first hire. The advising relationship didn’t end at closing — it’s still where they pressure-test the next decision.
