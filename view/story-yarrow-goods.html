Turning a pivot into a business.

After the pandemic closed their stores, Oliver and Grace started Yarrow Goods from a spare bedroom, selling ethically manufactured, screen-printed apparel.

Demand was growing. Inventory needed funding.

Preorders meant long waits. They needed capital for inventory and faster fulfillment.

“We would have closed our business if it were not for the help we received from the Marin SBDC.”

Yarrow Goods founders

The right person, in their corner.

Marin SBDC advisor Richard Strezo helped them examine their finances, prepare projections, and connect with lenders. Together, they made the business’s potential easier for funders to understand.

A stronger foundation for what’s next.

The reported results: $138,000 in funding and 20% sales growth, with plans to expand operations and products.

Adapted from Marin SBDC’s published client story. Results reflect that story’s reporting period.
