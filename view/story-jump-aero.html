A faster path to first flight.

How Jump Aero raised $12M to build the world’s fastest first-response aircraft — with an Entrepreneur-in-Residence at their side.

4 min read
The business Electric first-response aircraft, Petaluma
Worked with Tech Futures Group Entrepreneur-in-Residence
The outcome $12M raised · SBIR-ready · Flight testing funded
Cost to the business $0 — no cost, no equity

The story

[Draft copy — replace with the approved story.] Jump Aero is building an aircraft designed to put a first responder anywhere in a rural county within minutes. The engineering was world-class; the question was the one every deep-tech founder faces: how do you fund the runway to first flight?

They applied to Tech Futures Group and were paired with an Entrepreneur-in-Residence who had raised rounds before. Together they sharpened the financial projections, the IP story and the pitch — before ever sitting down with investors.

“Our EIR had sat on the other side of the table. We walked into every investor meeting knowing our numbers cold.”

The result: $12M raised and a funded path to flight testing. The relationship didn’t end at the raise — TFG remains where they pressure-test what comes next, at no cost and no equity.
