# MySBDC preview design requirements

The user's preferences override the older numbered editorial style in the repository.

- Never add decorative section numbers, numbered eyebrow labels, or 01/02/03 card markers.
- Never use hairline dividers or thin card borders. Separate content with spacing, background color, and typography. Subtle footer dividers are the only allowed exception.
- Avoid decorative arrows. Use clear action labels. Upload/download icons may communicate a real function.
- Use sans serif for sections, statistics, forms, and dashboard cards. Reserve serif for a small number of primary headlines or quotations.
- Put preview notices in the footer or the relevant simulation dialog, never over page content.
- Preserve the original designs outside `/view`.
- Integration endpoints and delivery estimates are proposals until confirmed by the development teams. Never present simulated API calls or document uploads as real.

- Keep the public navigation grouped toward the logo, with comfortable breathing room between them, a conventional text-color hover, and no pill background.
- The language control shows only a globe when closed; language names appear after opening its dropdown.

- Use `#091321` (the existing `--footer-ink` token) for the shared footer background on every public page.

- Frame advisor and client portraits with a substantial red bottom rule. Feature testimonials may also use a square pool-blue offset rectangle; avoid rounded photo corners. Keep people’s faces prominent in the crop.
- Constrain body copy to readable measures and keep supporting proof inside its relevant column. Public training cards use soft series colors, with dark text and clearly visible search controls.
