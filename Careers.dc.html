01 — Why here

Nothing to sell. Someone to help.

The work is real. Your clients are the bakery on the corner and the startup in the garage. You’ll see what your advice did, usually within the year.

University benefits. Employed through the Cal Poly Humboldt Sponsored Programs Foundation — health, retirement, paid leave.

Where you already live. Most roles are remote or hybrid within the region. We hire for the county, not just the headquarters.

Operators welcome. Many of our advisors came from running their own businesses. That experience counts here more than a résumé format.

03 — How hiring works

Three conversations, no gauntlet.

We aim to go from application to offer in about four weeks.

01 Apply. Résumé and a short note about the businesses you want to help. No cover-letter theater.
02 Talk with the team. A conversation with the center director and an advisor you’d work beside.
03 Work a real case. We walk through an anonymized client situation together. It’s the job, so it’s the interview.
