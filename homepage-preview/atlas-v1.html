      About this map

      This page is a design preview. The four network totals match the FY2025 draft figures on the impact page. Where each light sits, the county breakdowns, the dates and the quotes are sample content and need real reporting data before public release.

      A light shows a town and a kind of milestone. It never shows a person, a business name or an amount. Jobs and dollars appear only as totals for the region or a county, and a milestone is added once an SBDC advisor confirms it. Business owners choose whether their milestone appears here.

      Capital accessed, sales growth, jobs created, and businesses started are different measures and should not be added together.
