      About this map

      This page is a design preview. The four network totals match the FY2025 draft figures on the impact page. County and state outlines are simplified from US Census Bureau files. Where each mark sits, the county breakdowns and the dates are sample content and need real reporting data before public release.

      The two named success stories are existing client stories from the website draft, placed at their town or county. The three quotes on the map are sample content.

      A mark shows a town and a kind of milestone. It never shows a person, a business name or an amount. Jobs and dollars appear only as totals for the region or a county, and a milestone is added once an SBDC advisor confirms it. Business owners choose whether their milestone appears here, and a named story appears only with the owner’s agreement.

      Capital accessed, sales growth, jobs created, and businesses started are different measures and should not be added together.
