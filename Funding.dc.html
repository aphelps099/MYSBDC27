04 — Common questions

The things people ask before they ask.

Does this really cost nothing? Yes. Advising is funded by the U.S. Small Business Administration and the State of California. No fee, and we never take equity.
Will you share my financial information? No. Advising is confidential. Your financials are shared with a lender only when you choose to apply.
What if my credit isn’t great, or I’ve been turned down? That’s exactly what advising is for. Your advisor helps you understand what lenders look for, strengthen your position and build a realistic path to yes.
How long does it take? It depends on how lender-ready you are today — some clients meet a lender within weeks, others spend a few months getting financials in shape. Your advisor gives you an honest read at the first meeting.
