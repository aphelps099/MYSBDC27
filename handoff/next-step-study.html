Home · Your next step

One next step.
Every state.

The same sheet, a useful next action, and a clear voice. Only one state appears on the client’s Home at a time.

These are sample designs with product notes attached. Actions within the sheets are illustrated, not live controls.

What appears first

  1. Advisor’s noteAlways takes priority
  2. Documents waitingIf there is no active note
  3. Coming upIf no documents are waiting
  4. Caught upWhen nothing else applies

Done is a temporary receipt. A new advisor note overrides it. There is no milestone nudge.

Product notes

Show when
Dana’s note asks for an action the app cannot observe, such as a phone call.
Clear when
The client chooses “Mark as done” and MySBDC successfully saves the completion against this note version.
Receipt wording
“Done. You marked this step complete.” This acknowledges the client’s report; it does not claim a file was sent.

This quiet completion action appears only on eligible advisor notes.

Keep for
The rest of this visit. Do not immediately replace the success message with another task.

View session

Product notes

Show when
No active note or waiting document takes priority, and a booked session falls within the agreed reminder window.
Use real details
The actual appointment, time zone and available session destination. Do not imply a booking that does not exist.
Clear when
The session ends or is cancelled. Retire the reminder without a Done receipt or an attendance claim.

Product notes

Show when
There is no active advisor note, eligible waiting document, or upcoming event to show.
Keep the sheet
Do not hide it or invent another task. This is the real empty state, written as reassurance.
Replace when
A new eligible item arrives. Sending a message does not itself create a completion receipt.

No milestone nudge, profile-gap prompt, or inferred training suggestion.

Product notes

Show when
The client is signed up and still being matched. This precedes the active-client selection order.
No invented advisor
Use the center’s name. Do not show Dana’s photo or a “Message Dana” action before assignment.
Replace when
The assignment workflow confirms an advisor and the active-client experience can begin.

The matching timeframe follows the existing Home example; confirm it with center operations.

For implementation

What the system
needs to remember.

These designs illustrate the agreed behavior. They do not connect to account data or send notifications.

Complete the exact note
MySBDC stores the client, note ID and version, completion method, and time. Neoserra does not store this completion.
Confirm first, then acknowledge
Emumba must provide a supported upload/download signal for the matching request or document. Failed saves keep the step active; unrelated uploads do not clear it.
Notify without duplicates
The backend sends the proposed advisor email through Resend after saving completion. Retries must not send it twice. Notification failure must not undo success.
Agree the remaining rules
Define a “visit,” the upcoming-event window, download notification rules, and the center contact route before connecting production data.
