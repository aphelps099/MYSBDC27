Partners

Thank you
to our partners.

Thanks to our vast network of partners, we can act as a convener of resources for the Northern California business community.

Our advising is offered at no fee thanks to support from the U.S. Small Business Administration, the California Office of the Small Business Advocate and local partners.

Federal & state funding

Our work would not be possible without our federal and state funders. Their support lets us offer expert guidance to small businesses at no fee to them.

U.S. Small Business Administration

Created in 1953, the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) is the only cabinet-level federal agency fully dedicated to being the nation’s premier voice for small businesses.

Visit SBA’s website

California Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development

The Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development (GO-Biz) serves as the State of California’s leader for job growth, economic development, and business assistance efforts.

Visit GO-Biz’s website

Financial hosting

Cal Poly Humboldt

As the NorCal SBDC program host, Cal Poly Humboldt collaborates on regional and state-wide small business initiatives, providing services including grant and contract applications geared toward economic development.

Visit Cal Poly Humboldt

Service partners

Partner programs that offer specialized, no-cost services alongside our advising.

NorCal APEX Accelerators

NorCal APEX Accelerators (formerly known as NorCal PTAC) is a nonprofit program formed to help businesses successfully sell their products and services to government agencies. Their free services include one-on-one counseling, custom bid matching, training, resources, and networking opportunities.

Visit NorCal APEX

Pacific Northwest Regional VBOC

Formerly NorCal VBOC. Designed to be a no-cost, go-to resource for transitioning service members, veterans, and military spouses looking to start, purchase or grow a business, the Veterans Business Outreach Center provides business development assistance, training, counseling, mentoring, and resource referrals at no cost to clients.

Visit the VBOC website

Local partners

We are sincerely grateful to our generous local financial partners, who share our vision for a thriving, prosperous small business community throughout Northern California.

Better Business Bureau of Northeast California

BBB’s mission is to be the leader in advancing marketplace trust. We do this by setting standards, encouraging and supporting best practices by engaging with and educating consumers and businesses, celebrating marketplace role models, calling out and addressing substandard marketplace behavior, and creating a community of trustworthy businesses and charities throughout our communities.

Visit BBB’s website
