# Content audit — to do (opened November 27, 2026)

The site owner's call: a lot of the copy on the public pages is "content slop": extra sentences that repeat the
headline, the button or the next section, written to fill space rather than to tell someone something. Example
removed from the Funding hero: "New equipment. A bigger space. Room to grow. Work with a Finance Center advisor to
explore your options and prepare for financing." The headline and the button already said it.

## The sweep

Go page by page through `handoff/front/` (homepage, Advising, Specialty programs, Training, Funding, Stories, story
pages, News, About, Partners, Impact) and for every block of copy ask:

1. Does it say something the headline, button or neighbouring section does not? If not, cut it.
2. Is it a fact (a figure, a name, a place, a program, a requirement) or filler? Keep facts; cut filler.
3. Hero and section intros: a headline plus one short line at most, often none. No three-sentence lead-ins.
4. No repeated promises: "confidential, no fee" belongs once near the main action, not under every heading.
5. Lists of "we help with…" adjectives, mirrored phrases ("Big decisions. Everyday details.") and stacked fragments
   need a real job on the page or they go.
6. Captions say who and where. Nothing else.

Report what was cut, page by page, before deleting anything larger than a sentence or two, and keep every real fact.
