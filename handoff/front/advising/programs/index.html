Specialty programs

Specialist support,
when you need it.

Four programs add specialist help to SBDC advising: preparing for financing, building a tech venture, and winning government and corporate contracts.

SBDC Finance Center

Prepare for financing with a Finance Center advisor.

Work on your financial statements, projections and loan package, and explore SBA and bank loans, equipment and real estate financing, grants and other funding.

Explore funding
  • SBA & bank loans
  • Equipment & real estate
  • Buying or selling a business
  • Grants & other funding

$250M+in working capital accessed by clients since 2018, as reported by the Finance Center.

Tech Futures Group

A venture platform for Northern California tech startups.

TFG gives Northern California tech startups the resources and strategy to move faster and build bigger. Its services are offered at no cost and take no equity.

Explore Tech Futures Group
  • Business strategy
  • Specialist advice
  • Fundraising and grants
  • No cost, no equity

$700M+raised by founders TFG has helped, plus over $68M in grants and more than 2,700 jobs created.

ProBiz

Grow by winning state, municipal and corporate contracts.

ProBiz advisors guide you through certification, help you secure the funding to fulfil contracts and connect you with buyers. Every service is offered at no cost.

Explore ProBiz
  • Certification assistance
  • Bid preparation & review
  • Quarterly contracting training
  • Connections with buyers

Norcal APEX Accelerator

Free counseling to compete in the government marketplace.

Procurement specialists help you decide if you are ready for local, state and federal contracting, set you up with custom bid matching and share training and resources.

Visit Norcal APEX Accelerator
  • One-on-one counseling
  • Custom bid matching
  • Training & webinars
  • Local, state & federal

870+clients helped to win more than $500 million in government contracts last year.
