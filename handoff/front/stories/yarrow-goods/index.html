Yarrow
Goods.

From a spare bedroom to a growing apparel business. Grace and Oliver Estrada found the funding—and the support—to keep going.

Grace & Oliver Estrada · Yarrow Goods

Ecommerce · Marin SBDC

A new business.
A way forward.

Visit Yarrow Goods

When the pandemic closed their two shops, Grace and Oliver began Yarrow Goods in 2020, combining retail experience and screen-printing skills.

Online sales gained traction, but buying inventory required capital. Pre-orders meant long waits for customers.

Someone in their corner.

Marin SBDC advisor Richard Strezo helped them examine their numbers, strengthen their case for lenders and make funding connections. He became a sounding board as they worked through the decisions ahead.

Room to grow.

Results reported in the published client story.

Funding secured
$138K
Increase in sales
20%

Financing came from five sources, including Pacific Community Ventures and TMC Community Capital.
