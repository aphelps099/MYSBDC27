Small business.
Lasting impact.

A shop opening its doors. A maker hiring their first employee. A founder turning years of work into something the world hasn’t seen before. Northern California’s economy is built one business at a time.

Behind each business is a person making decisions, taking chances, and building a future. NorCal SBDC helps them take the next step with no-fee, confidential advising and practical business training.

From neighborhood storefronts to food producers and technology companies, the work looks different in every community. So does the support each owner needs.

We help entrepreneurs work through the decisions that matter: how to fund a new idea, reach more customers, manage cash flow, or build a business that can keep growing.

When a small business moves forward, a whole community moves with it.

The right support.
A different possibility.

Good advice starts with listening. Our advisors work alongside business owners to understand what’s happening now, what they want to achieve, and what it will take to get there.

A stronger business plan. A clearer path to financing. A connection made at the right moment. This is the everyday work behind the results—and the beginning of what comes next.

How advising works

Real progress.
In people’s own lives.

For Grace and Oliver Estrada of Yarrow Goods, growing an apparel business meant finding a way to fund inventory. Working with Marin SBDC advisor Richard Strezo, they sharpened their financial story and connected with lenders.

Their published success story records $138,000 in financing across five sources and a 20% increase in sales. Behind those numbers: a family business with room to move forward.
