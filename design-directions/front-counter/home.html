How advising works

No fee. Funded by the SBA and the State of California.

  1. Tell us about your business.

    Complete a client profile. It’s the same form for every program, so you only fill it in once.

  2. Meet your advisor.

    Your local center matches you with an advisor who works on plans, funding, marketing and operations.

  3. Keep going in MySBDC.

    Your next step, your documents and your workshops, in one place.
