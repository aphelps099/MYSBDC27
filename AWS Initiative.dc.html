05 — Terms of service · v1-1.14.26 +

The short version.

Our role is advisory — we help you figure out what you need and connect you with vetted partners. We don’t build the technology ourselves, and we can’t guarantee outcomes. But we’ll do our best to point you in the right direction.

Last updated January 2026 · NorCal SBDC Lead Center, 876 7th Street, Arcata, CA 95501

What we do No-cost business advising · technology readiness assessment · partner matching · support through the process.
What we don’t do Implement technology directly · guarantee funding or approval · provide legal, tax or financial advice · warrant third-party work.
Eligibility Small businesses in the NorCal SBDC service area (36 counties). Partner criteria may also apply — meeting an advisor doesn’t guarantee acceptance.
Partner funding Provided by participating AWS partners — not by the SBDC or AWS directly. Not guaranteed, may be less than $25,000, and applies to implementation services rather than cash.

Advisory services are provided “as is,” with no warranty as to outcomes, partner performance or funding approval. Your relationship with any AWS partner is between you and that partner. Information you share may be provided to AWS, participating partners, and our host institution and funding agencies for program and reporting purposes; we do not sell your information.
